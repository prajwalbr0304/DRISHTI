Renukaswamy case: public evidence source pack

Prepared 27 August 2026 · Companion data: evidence_manifest.csv

Not a police evidence dump. The actual CCTV masters, forensic phone images, FSL reports, post-mortem material, CDRs, witness statements and marked trial exhibits remain with police/court. Media files below are publisher-hosted renditions and cannot establish forensic authenticity or chain of custody.
All accused are presumed innocent unless convicted after trial. Label every allegation as the prosecution's case. Do not present a bail order, media clip or photograph as proof of guilt.

Classification

A — public judicial record Primary court text, although the Indian Kanoon copy is not a certified copy.

B — editorial photograph/video Publisher-attributable and useful for context, but copyrighted and not trial evidence.

C — media-published evidence extract An edited/compressed copy attributed to case evidence; not the original.

D/E — unavailable or excluded Nonpublic trial records, graphic leaks, private data or unverifiable reposts.

F — public geodata reference A locality-level map reference, not an exact alleged incident-scene coordinate.

Original materials not publicly obtainable

  • Complete CCTV master exports with native timestamps, codecs, metadata and acquisition logs.
  • Forensic phone images and complete device-extraction reports.
  • FSL/CFSL DNA, serology, soil and electronic-forensic reports and annexures.
  • Certified post-mortem report and autopsy material.
  • Complete CDRs, tower-location records and authenticated Instagram/WhatsApp exports.
  • Full witness statements, depositions, prosecution exhibits P1–P13, material objects MO1–MO8 and defence markings D1–D22.
  • The authenticated 3,991-page main charge sheet and both supplementary charge sheets.
For genuine originals, an authorized party or counsel would need to seek inspection or certified copies through the trial court/police process. Access may be restricted because the trial is pending, the records contain private information, and some material is graphic.

Presentation rules

  1. Keep the publisher name and source URL beside every visual.
  2. Use “media-published CCTV extract attributed by the prosecution,” never “original CCTV proof.”
  3. Do not crop out timestamps, captions, watermarks or contextual labels.
  4. Do not use leaked body, assault or autopsy images.
  5. Do not expose telephone numbers, addresses, private chats or images of minors.
  6. State prominently that the criminal trial is pending and no guilt finding is established by these materials.
