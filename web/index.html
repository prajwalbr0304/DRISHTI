See more clearly. Act more accountably.

The decision intelligence platform for Karnataka State Police — bringing statewide posture, case intelligence, geography and emergency response onto one governed foundation.

Fragmented signals become coordinated understanding

Public-safety information arrives in pieces: station records, evidence stores, jurisdiction boundaries, maps and spreadsheets. The hard part was never collecting more data. It is understanding what matters together, fast enough to act on.

DRISHTI connects those pieces without handing judgement to a machine. Sources stay visible, uncertainty stays stated, and a named officer remains responsible for what happens next. Every analytical output carries its source, confidence, scope and review status.

Powerful intelligence. Clear boundaries.

Predictions are labelled. Uncertainty stays visible. Sensitive records stay inside role and jurisdiction boundaries. DRISHTI strengthens professional judgement, and makes every step inspectable afterwards.

  • Source-aware by default

    Every figure, relationship and forecast resolves back to the records it came from.

  • Scoped to role and jurisdiction

    Access follows the command structure and is enforced at the trusted boundary.

  • Human review before action

    Analysis supports a decision; it never becomes the decision-maker.

  • Accountable after the moment

    Queries, model versions, evidence and decisions stay in a complete audit trail.

Human authority is never optional. No analytical output creates an operational consequence on its own. Predictive output is decision support and must not be read as automatic enforcement.

This is a prototype. Every record in this demonstration is synthetic. It is not a production criminal-information system and must not be interpreted as one.
